My goddess:

The **starknife** is Desna's favored weapon, and Desnan clerics are famous for using it. It fits her themes of wandering, stars, and freedom. Many followers decorate their starknives with butterfly or star motifs.

A few roleplaying notes that might help:

- Desnans tend to be optimistic and encouraging.
- They often enjoy travel and exploration.
- They value freedom highly and dislike controlling others.
- They are known for trusting intuition and dreams.
- They often help lost travelers, both literally and metaphorically.

One fun detail is that Desna is one of the most commonly worshipped good deities by adventurers because "go see what's beyond the horizon" is basically her philosophy.

So if you're a healing priest throwing a starknife around, you're playing a very classic Desnan cleric. 😊
